# Community conduct

We welcome respectful participation from people of all backgrounds. Treat contributors with care, give constructive technical feedback, and respect privacy.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexual harassment, and publication of another person's private information are unacceptable in project discussions and contributions.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate contributions, warn participants, or restrict participation for repeated or serious violations. Decisions should be proportionate and consider the context. Affected participants may request reconsideration from the repository owner.

For non-sensitive conduct issues, contact the repository owner through GitHub. Do not publish private evidence in public issues. For vulnerabilities, follow [the security policy](docs/SECURITY.md).
